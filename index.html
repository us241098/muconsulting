What we do

Four ways we put AI to work in your business

Start with one. Most businesses begin with the thing that costs them the most hours every week.

Voice AI

Voicebots

A phone assistant that picks up every call, day or night, and talks to customers in Hindi or English.

  • Takes bookings, orders and appointments
  • Calls back leads and follows up on payments
  • Sends a summary of every call to your WhatsApp
On-site

AI consulting

We visit your shop, office or factory, watch how work actually gets done, and tell you plainly where AI helps and where it doesn't.

  • A walk-through of your daily operations
  • A short written plan, ranked by time and money saved
  • Honest advice, even when the answer is "not yet"
n8n

Custom workflows

We connect the tools you already use so data moves by itself, without anyone copying it from one screen to another.

  • WhatsApp orders straight into Google Sheets or Tally
  • Automatic invoices, reminders and daily reports
  • Built on n8n, so you own it and it stays cheap to run
Factory floor

Industrial automation

Camera-based systems that count, check and log what moves on your production line.

  • Bottle, box and unit counters for any conveyor
  • Spot damaged or mislabelled items
  • Live production numbers on your phone
Examples of what we build

What this looks like in an Udaipur business

Typical problems we see and the system we would build for each. These are examples, not client case studies.

Lakeside hotel

Voicebot
The problem

Guests call at midnight to ask about rooms. Nobody picks up, and they book on a travel site instead.

What we build

A voicebot that answers every call, checks availability, quotes the rate and sends the booking to the front desk on WhatsApp.

Marble & stone unit

Industrial automation
The problem

Tiles and slabs leaving the line are counted by hand, and the totals rarely match dispatch.

What we build

A camera over the conveyor that counts every piece and shows shift totals on the owner's phone.

Clinic

Voicebot + workflow
The problem

Patients forget appointments, and the receptionist spends the morning on reminder calls.

What we build

Automatic WhatsApp and call reminders the day before, with replies updating the appointment sheet.

Handicraft shop

n8n workflow
The problem

Orders arrive on WhatsApp and Instagram, then get typed into a register and Tally by hand.

What we build

A workflow that collects every order into one sheet, creates the invoice and sends it to the customer.

Restaurant

Voicebot
The problem

Weekend table bookings jam the phone line while staff are busy serving.

What we build

A booking assistant that takes reservations by phone and keeps a live table list for the manager.
